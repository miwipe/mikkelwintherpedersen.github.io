Topics I speak about

From public lectures to scientific conferences, my talks draw on research into past nature and the traces it leaves behind.

A lost Greenland

The discovery of two-million-year-old DNA and the plants and animals that once lived in a much warmer Greenland.

Nature’s genetic archive

How DNA survives in soil and lake sediments, how we recover it, and what it can tell us about ecosystems that no longer exist.

People, climate and biodiversity

What ancient environmental DNA reveals about the relationship between human activity, changing climates and biodiversity through time.
